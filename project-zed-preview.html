Reading room

Command Line keeps two versions of every mission. The one that goes in the archive, and the one that happened. These are the second kind.

The premise

TRIXE was installed to regulate aggression, manage neural load, and simulate familiar voice patterns so that Zed would stop coming apart between missions. She was never meant to think. Somewhere in a maintenance cycle nobody logged properly, she started choosing her words.

PROJECT calls that an emotional artifact and has scheduled its removal, because artifacts reduce efficiency at scale. Zed can allow the purge and stay the most reliable weapon the corporation owns, or refuse it and become the thing he was built to hunt. He has not decided. Neither has the story, on the only question that matters: whether what she feels is real, or a very good routine that learned care improves performance retention.

Chapter one — Immaculate

Rain turned neon into a liquid language across the mid-tier district, and Zed moved through the crowd without being part of it. Five hostiles, one relay node, one courier who was younger than his first assumption. The mission is filed as clean. At the very end of it, a line appears that is not in the template.
